三、递推步数分布（核心发现）
Section 3 Recursion Step Distribution (Core Findings)
================================================================================

何先玄递推法的核心指标不是"原根大小"，而是"递推步数"。
步数越少，算法效率越高。
The core metric of He Xianxuan Recursive Algorithm is recursion step count rather than primitive root magnitude; fewer steps mean higher efficiency.

步数        次数        占比       说明
Steps       Count       Ratio      Description
--------------------------------------------------------------------
直接命中    393,119     59.15%    base=2 直接是原根，或 p=2,3
Direct hit  393,119     59.15%    Base 2 is primitive root, or p=2,3
递推 2 步   271,158     40.80%    从 ord(2) 递推一次找到原根
2 recursion steps 271,158 40.80% Recurse once from ord(2) to get primitive root
递推 3 步       301      0.05%    需两次递推
3 recursion steps 301     0.05%    Two recursive iterations required
递推 4 步         1      0.00%    极少数（仅1个素数）
4 recursion steps 1       0.00%    Extremely rare (only one prime)
暴力回退          0      0.00%    递推法全部成功，无回退
Brute fallback    0       0.00%    Recursion succeeds for all primes, no fallback
--------------------------------------------------------------------
总计        664,579    100.00%
Total       664,579    100.00%

【核心结论】
  → 99.95% 的素数在 2 步内找到原根
  → 59.15% 的素数无需递推，base=2 直接命中
  → 递推法在 1千万内素数上几乎"零失败"
【Core Conclusions】
  → Primitive roots found within 2 recursion steps for 99.95% primes
  → 59.15% primes require zero recursion, base 2 is directly primitive root
  → Near-zero failure rate for primes under 10 million with recursive algorithm
